Frequently asked questions

Do I need a dough mixer and a sheeter?+

In the class the dough is mixed in a mixer and rolled in a sheeter, like in our production. At home you can knead by hand and use a rolling pin: it changes the effort, not the folding technique.

Is the class in English?+

Yes. The narration was dubbed into English using AI with Izadora’s own voice, and the whole class has English subtitles and on-screen steps.
